READER MODE — a warm, paper-like screen filter for your Mac
============================================================

INSTALL
1. Drag "ReaderMode" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click Reader Mode.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
Your Mac will say it "can't verify" the app, because it isn't from the App Store.
That's normal for apps shared between friends. To allow it:
  1. Click "Done" (or "OK") on that message.
  2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to Reader Mode.
  4. Enter your Mac password if asked, then click "Open Anyway" again.
You only have to do this once.

USING IT
- Look for the book icon at the top right of your screen (in the menu bar).
- Click it to switch Reader Mode on and adjust Contrast, Warmth and Brightness.
- Four slots (Favorite, #2, #3, #4): pick one, adjust the sliders, press Save.
- Control + Option + R turns it on/off from anywhere.
- Tick "Open at login" to start it automatically.

It doesn't change any system settings. Quit the app and your screen goes
straight back to normal. To uninstall, quit it and drag it to the Trash.

Works on macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
