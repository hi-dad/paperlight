PAPERLIGHT — a warm, paper-like screen filter for your Mac
============================================================

INSTALL
1. Drag "Paperlight" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
2. Open Applications and double-click Paperlight.

THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT
Your Mac asks if you're sure you want to open an app from the internet.
Click "Open". The app is signed and checked by Apple.

USING IT
- Look for the book icon at the top right of your screen (in the menu bar).
- Click it to switch Paperlight on and adjust Contrast, Warmth and Brightness.
- Four slots (Favorite, #2, #3, #4): pick one, adjust the sliders, press Save.
- Control + Option + R turns it on/off from anywhere.
- Tick "Open at login" to start it automatically.

It doesn't change any system settings. Quit the app and your screen goes
straight back to normal. To uninstall, quit it and drag it to the Trash.

Works on macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer, on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
